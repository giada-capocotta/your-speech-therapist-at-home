# Your Speech Therapist at Home

Your Speech Therapist at Home is an independent concept for an evidence-based digital platform designed to support speech and language therapists in creating personalised home intervention programmes.

The platform would integrate standardized assessment with intervention resources, allowing clinicians to select activities based on each patient's individual needs and enabling families to continue treatment at home.
